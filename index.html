Where the data comes from, and how accurate it is
  • Boundary: survey distances and bearings on the site plans. They agree with the council parcel to within 10 mm.
  • Ground: Auckland Council 2024 LiDAR contours (0.25 m) from an Auckland Council GeoMaps extract, interpolated to a 0.5 m grid; the contour lines shown are drawn from that grid. Levels are converted to the datum of the consent plans as RL = NZVD2016 + 79.9 m, from the garage floor (97.331) and the 2010 sections; about ±0.3 m. Ground under the house is interpolated.
  • House: 2010 consent floor plan. Its angle matches the council building footprint. Its position uses the Site & Stormwater Plan offsets (7.25 m and 1.29 m; 3.25 m at the rear), because the council footprint and aerial show roofs leaning about 1.2 m north-west. Expect ±0.3 m.
  • Heights: walls to RL 102.45 and a 15.7° hip roof, both measured off Section Y-Y. Roof layout from the hip and ridge lines on the 2010 floor plan, which match the aerial photo: one long north–south ridge over the bedroom wing, hipped at the road end; an east–west ridge over the lounge wing, hipped at the east end; one shared rear roof plane over the lounge and dining; and a separate hipped roof over the family room.
  • Deck: 2010 outline, which the aerial still matches, plus a correction by the owner: the deck's east edge continues the lounge wing's east wall line about 1.55 m south, then runs diagonally to the deck's south-east corner (scaled from a mark-up by the owner, ±0.2 m). Stairs: open treads along the south edge, with a square landing at deck level at the west end, then stairs down to the east, finishing at the deck's south-east corner post; assumed 1.0 m wide, with risers and treads spaced evenly to fit.
  • Wastewater main and water pipe: council GIS. The GIS labels the main 100 mm AC, not the 150 mm PVC on the older plan. Confirm with a locate.
  • West-side windows: from Elevation A, ±0.15 m; the ensuite end of the elevation was cut off, so no window is shown there. Other windows and outside doors: positions from the 2010 floor plans (±0.15 m), checked against photos taken in 2026. Where the photos differ from the plans, the model follows the photos: the road-end window of bedroom 1 is about 0.5 m wider than on the plan, bedroom 1 has a sliding door onto the verandah, the lounge has a sliding door about 2.5 m wide onto the rear deck where the plan shows a window, the lounge windows on the driveway side reach the floor, and two lower windows under the rear deck (into the garage and the new bedroom) are not on the plans. Only the heights of the bedroom 4 south window are assumed. Lower floor: ground under the house is cut down to the lower slab from the retaining wall 4.45 m in from the road-end wall (Section X-X); north of it the subfloor stays at natural ground.
  • Sun: position calculated for Red Beach (36.6° S, 174.7° E) from the date and NZ clock time, including daylight saving. The model's north is grid north, within about 2° of true north.
  • Cladding and the garage door: brick base and boarded upper walls follow Elevation A; the garage door's width and position are assumed (2.4 m, centred on the single garage). Verandah posts and steps follow the 2010 floor plan.
  • Doors: positions and swings from the 2010 floor plans, ±0.15 m, plus the hall-to-garage door from a mark-up by the owner. Wardrobe and cupboard doors are not shown. Not on the drawings, so assumed: the door from the downstairs hall into the new bedroom, and the garage door. The ensuite has an opening but no door is drawn on the plan.
  • Rooms: 2010 consent floor plans, with the owner's changes (single garage, hall and new bedroom downstairs; WC joined to the bathroom: the wall between them shortened to just past the vanity, the WC's hall door replaced by wall, and the shower removed). Interior walls were re-traced at higher resolution against the plan's overall dimensions, ±0.15 m. Walls are cut at 1.2 m above each floor. Areas are approximate, measured to wall lines.
  • Existing driveway, proposed corridor and street tree: traced from a mark-up by the owner of the older site plan, so ±0.3 m.
